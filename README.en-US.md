### My Open Source Life with My Wife

My wife [Vanessa](https://github.com/Vanessa219) and I have been writing open source software since 2009, and the Solo project we have been maintaining has now spanned 10 years. Our creative focus mainly revolves around blog and community systems, and we have been working towards the [B3log Concept - Distributed Community Network](https://ld246.com/article/1546941897596).

In early 2018, Vanessa and I left our company and officially began our entrepreneurial journey as "full-time open source developers and freelancers." We established a company whose primary product is the Sym community system; its [community edition](https://github.com/88250/symphony) is completely open source and free for individuals to use based on its open source license. Additionally, we operate a community called [Liandi](https://ld246.com) with over 50,000 users, serving as a community node for the B3log distributed community network.

Recently, we started a new project, [SiYuan Note](https://github.com/siyuan-note/siyuan), a local offline-first personal knowledge management system that supports fine-grained block-level referencing and Markdown WYSIWYG. Everyone is welcome to try it out and provide feedback.

For Vanessa and me, open source is no longer just a hobby, but a way of life. We are full of confidence in the future of this "open source life" path. We hope that we can help others through open source software along this road, and that others can help us in return.

Open source connects you and me; open source builds the future. Let's enter the world of open source together!
